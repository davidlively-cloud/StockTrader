Plain-words glossary

VWAP
Volume-weighted average price: the average price paid for every share traded today. Buyers are in control while price stays above it.
Relative volume
Shares traded so far today compared with a normal day by this time. 2x means twice the usual activity, a sign the stock is "in play".
ATR
Average true range: how far the stock usually moves in a day, from the last 14 days. Stops and "too stretched" are measured in ATRs so they fit each stock.
EMA 9 / 20
Smoothed averages of the last 9 and 20 five-minute closes. The short one above the long one means the short-term trend is up.
Stop and R
The stop is the price where you sell to cap the loss. R is the money at risk between entry and stop; a 2R target aims to win twice what you risk.
PM high / low
Highest and lowest prices traded before the 9:30 open. Breaking the PM high is bullish; falling under the PM low means the gap failed.
